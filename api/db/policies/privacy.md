Tåno + Tåsi collects only what it needs to run this store. We don't sell your personal information.

## What we collect

- **When you order:** your email, name, shipping address, and what you bought. Card details go directly to Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
- **When you join the list:** your email address.
- **When you email us:** your message and address.
- **On your device:** your cart is saved in your browser so it's still there when you come back. Our servers also keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the store running and secure.

We don't use advertising trackers on this site.

## How we use it

We use your information to fill orders, send order and shipping emails, answer questions, prevent fraud, enforce drop limits, and keep required records. If you join the list, we'll email you about new releases, and every email lets you leave.

## Who helps us

We share what's needed with the companies that run parts of this store:

- Stripe, for payments
- EasyPost and shipping carriers, for rates, labels and delivery
- Resend, for email
- Render, Neon, Netlify and Amazon Web Services, for hosting, data and images
- Clerk, for store staff sign-in

We may also share information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for tax, accounting and legal reasons. You can leave the email list any time.

## Your choices

Email {{contact_email}} to see, correct or delete your information, or to ask how it's used. We'll honor the rights your state or territory gives you, including under California law, and we won't treat you differently for asking. Some records may need to stay for legal reasons.

## Children

This store is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

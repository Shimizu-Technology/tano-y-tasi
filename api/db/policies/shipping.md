Every order ships from the United States.

## Where we ship

We ship to all 50 states, Guam, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, American Samoa, the Northern Mariana Islands, and APO/FPO/DPO addresses. We don't ship internationally yet.

## Rates and timing

You'll see your shipping options and the exact cost at checkout, before you pay.

In-stock orders usually leave within 5 business days. Delivery time depends on the carrier and your destination, and shipments to Guam and the other islands can take longer. Preorders ship around the date shown on the product page. If that date changes, we'll email you and you can cancel for a full refund.

## Tracking

We'll email your tracking number when your order ships.

## Lost or damaged packages

Check that your address is right before you pay. If your package is lost, or arrives damaged, email {{contact_email}} and we'll make it right with a replacement or a refund.

## Duties and taxes

We only ship within U.S. territory, so there are no customs duties.

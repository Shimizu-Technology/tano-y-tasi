We want you to love what you ordered.

## Damaged items and missing pieces

If your order arrives damaged, or a puzzle is missing pieces, email {{contact_email}} within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement, or refund you if we can't, and we'll cover the shipping. This applies to drops too, as long as we have one left.

## Changed your mind

You can return an unopened item in its original condition within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item's price. Return shipping is on you unless we made a mistake, and original shipping costs aren't refundable. Email {{contact_email}} first so we can tell you where to send it.

A puzzle that has been opened can't be returned unless it was damaged or incomplete.

## Preorders

You can cancel a preorder for a full refund any time before it ships. If we can't ship by the date we gave you, we'll email you with a new date and you can cancel for a full refund.

## How refunds work

Refunds go back to your original payment method. Once we issue one, it can take 5 to 10 business days to appear on your statement.
